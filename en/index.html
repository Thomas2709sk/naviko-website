Why Naviko ?

Getting around town is still a challenge today for many visually impaired people. Despite existing aids like the white cane or guide dog, certain environmental details remain difficult to pick up quickly. Naviko was designed as a digital companion to complement these aids, offering greater peace of mind and independence on the move.

Your travel companion

Naviko is a mobile app designed to support visually impaired people on the move. Powered by artificial intelligence and the smartphone’s camera, the app identifies key environmental features—such as pedestrian crossings, obstacles, or traffic lights—and alerts the user through audio or haptic feedback.

Developed by a visually impaired creator, Naviko is built with a strong focus on accessibility, simplicity, and everyday needs.

Features

Smart detection

Naviko uses the smartphone camera and artificial intelligence to analyze the environment and identify key features while on the move.

Smart detection for your surroundings

Using artificial intelligence, Naviko can recognize key surroundings like pedestrian crossings, traffic lights, obstacles, vehicles, and pedestrians. This analysis provides context-aware assistance tailored to real-time situations.

Anticipating obstacles

Potential hazards that could disrupt or jeopardize travel are detected to quickly alert the user. Whether it is a vehicle, a temporary obstacle, or street furniture, Naviko helps make journeys smoother and more reassuring.

Assistance that adapts to you

Naviko keeps you informed in real time with clear voice alerts and subtle vibrations. Essential information is delivered without distracting you from your surroundings, supporting you every step of the way.

How to use Naviko ?

User guide

First use

When you first open Naviko, you will be asked to grant camera access and accept the terms of use to support you safely on every journey.

*Camera access and acceptance of the terms of use are required to use Naviko’s features.

Real-time analysis

Naviko analyzes your surroundings in real time to detect pedestrian crossings, traffic lights, pedestrians, and major obstacles.

Detection performance may vary depending on lighting conditions and surroundings.

Customize Naviko

Adjust the voice, contrast, text size, and accessibility settings to tailor Naviko to your needs.

Settings can be changed at any time from the app menu.

[Important] Using Naviko safely

Naviko is designed to support visually impaired individuals on their journeys. The app serves as a complementary aid and is not a substitute for user vigilance, a white cane, a guide dog, or any other mobility device.

  • Always point the camera forward to ensure optimal performance.
  • Use the app in well-lit conditions whenever possible
  • Make sure your smartphone battery is sufficiently charged before setting off.
  • Some detections may be less accurate depending on the environment, weather, or lighting conditions.
  • Always remain in full control of your movement and stay aware of your surroundings.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Naviko free ?

Yes, the app is free to download with its essential features.

Which smartphones does it work on ?

Naviko is currently only available on Android smartphones.

Does Naviko replace a white cane or a guide dog ?

No, Naviko is a complementary assistance tool and does not replace traditional mobility aids.

How to report an issue ?

You can report a bug directly from the « Support » menu in the app or via our contact email.

Are my data recorded?

No, no data is recorded. The AI operates 100% locally on your device, with no servers or data collection.
